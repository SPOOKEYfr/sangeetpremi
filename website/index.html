Search an artist. Discover their music. Enjoy everything in one place.

Type a singer, band or musician. SangeetPremi finds their songs across YouTube — official releases, soundtracks, collaborations, duets, live, acoustic, remixes — classifies every result, removes duplicates, and turns it all into an organized playlist.

What it does

One search, a complete picture of an artist

SangeetPremi reads public YouTube metadata through the official YouTube Data API v3, then does the organizing work for you: relevance scoring, 14 categories, role detection (primary, featured, playback singer), smart duplicate removal and honest filters.

♪

Artist discovery

Disambiguation when several artists share a name, relevance scoring, and classification into official, live, remix, duet, soundtrack and more.

✓

Smart duplicates

Strict, balanced or off — duplicates are collapsed, reviewable and restorable, never silently deleted.

☰

Playlist builder

Select, sort, save, duplicate, export as JSON, play on YouTube, or publish a real playlist to your own YouTube account (optional OAuth).

👥

Multi-artist

Comma-separated artists combined into one collection with correct per-track attribution.

🔍

Honest filters

Category, language (heuristics clearly labelled) and release-year range verified from real data — never guessed.

🔒

Privacy-first

No tracking, no analytics, no secrets in code. Your library stays in your browser's local storage and is exportable.
